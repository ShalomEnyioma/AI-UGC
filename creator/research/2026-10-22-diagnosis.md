# Diagnosis — Lane, Topics and Risks (2026-10-22)

This is a first-pass diagnosis based on web research.
The data scan of creators (vidIQ outlier search on TikTok and Instagram) is still pending: the connected vidIQ account showed 0 credits on the free plan when this was written.

## 1. Verdict on the "contrast" plan

### Pretending to be broke so people drag you: **No.**
- You sell courses, AI products and websites. If people find out the "broke girl" act was fake, you lose more than the video: buyers stop trusting you. Nigerians save and dig up receipts.
- Your ads for the teaching business are public, so someone *will* find them.
- **What to do instead: the delayed-receipt drop.**
  - Video 1 makes the claim with no proof ("I've never collected a kobo from a man I'm dating").
  - Let the comments demand receipts. Pin the angriest one.
  - Video 2 replies to that comment: "You asked. Here's how a teacher in Port Harcourt pays her own rent."
  - You still get the drag and the comment volume. The payoff turns haters into followers instead of exposing you as a liar.

### Preaching "don't sleep around" while living differently: **No.**
- A values brand only works if people believe you. Buchi survives every fight because his positions match his life. Hypocrisy is the one thing the Nigerian internet never forgives.
- **Your real thesis is different, and stronger: don't sleep with a man *for money*.**
  - It matches your life exactly: you choose who you want and collect nothing.
  - It offends both sides at once, which is what makes it controversial without being fake:
    - the church crowd, because you're not preaching chastity;
    - the "men must spend" crowd, Saida Boj's side, because you're attacking the money.
- Your private life stays private. You don't have to disclose it, and you mustn't lie about it.

## 2. Why she "can't hold a strong opinion"
- **Diagnosis:** She has opinions. What she doesn't have yet is *public stakes*: she hasn't said them where people can argue back, so they feel soft. Being open-minded about *people* is fine. A brand needs firmness about *principles*.
- **Evidence:** In one message she produced at least five clear positions:
  1. Don't collect money from a man you're dating.
  2. Dating shouldn't drag past about a year without a decision.
  3. Women can make the first move.
  4. Don't stay with someone because you're scared of being alone.
  5. People will leave you for someone with a bigger name, so build your own name.
- **The fix — opinion mining exercise.** Every day, write one line per prompt:
  - "Everybody says ___ but I think ___."
  - "The advice I was given at 19 that turned out to be a lie: ___."
  - "What I'd tell my younger sister about money and men: ___."
  - Keep only the ones you'd defend for 60 seconds with a real story. Those become videos.

## 4. Risk sheet — naming celebrities
- Section 24 of the Cybercrimes Act has been used against TikTokers who criticised people online. In June 2026 one TikToker (Immunizer) was remanded over posts about Mohbad's widow.
- Rules:
  - Attack statements, never the person's body, family or anything you can't prove.
  - Never make an allegation without evidence.
  - Never target private individuals.
  - React to clips through TikTok's own Stitch or Duet, rather than downloading and reposting, to reduce copyright strikes.

## 5. Format that fits her values
**Controversial hook, then a value-packed body.**
- Her values are teaching values: she says the well must replenish whoever drinks from it.
- Pure rage-bait doesn't fit that, and it doesn't sell courses.
- So: the first 3 seconds start a fight, and the next 45 seconds teach something useful.
- That's what separates a Buchi-style creator from someone who only collects drama.

## 6. Ladder from content to money
TikTok content → free WhatsApp Channel or Telegram community → paid community → courses and AI products.
Never use the phrase "make money online". Position it as **"skills a woman can earn from without a man"**: teaching, ads, AI tools.

## 7. Capacity
- Work 7 + sleep 7 + transport 2 + meals 2 = 18 hours, which leaves about 6 hours a day.
- Plan **15–20 hours a week**: 2 batch shoots, 1 post a day, 30 minutes a day answering comments.
- Don't run on 4 hours of sleep. It shows on camera, and consistency beats intensity.

## 8. First 7 videos
1. "I'm 26 and I've never collected money from a man I'm dating. Here's why." (Stitch a "men must spend" clip.)
