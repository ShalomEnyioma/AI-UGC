## 3. Format verdicts
1. **Split-screen reaction plus a verdict monologue, 60–120s.** This is the community builder. It's the Eric/Buchi template.
2. **Opinion bombs, 11–25s, a single take straight to camera.** These are reach engines: faith_succexx at 11s got 801K; imo at 21s got 1.4M.
3. **Comedy skits and dances:** not her lane. They get views but not authority.
