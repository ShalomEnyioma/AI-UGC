# Series 01 — "I don't collect money from the man I date"

**Status:** the title is waiting on Peace's pick. Recommended: **Zero Kobo From Him**.

## Why a series
- Every episode makes people come back for the next one.
- Each day is a receipt for the claim, so you never have to "prove it" in a single video.
- It's lifestyle content with a point, in the spirit of Passive Income Anna's vlogs. The difference is that every episode answers "how did today get paid for?"

## What the gap check found (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook — 22 Oct 2026)
- **The slogan is not new.** Facebook is full of small pages and pastors posting "Stop collecting money from men." Most are conditional: "…from men you don't love" or "…if you won't visit them."
- **A man, GehGeh, preaches it too.** It backfired publicly when his own sister called him out (amebo9janews, poshjournal). That's proof that hypocrisy gets exposed. **Live it before you preach it.**
- **Nobody is documenting it.** I found no woman running a daily, receipt-backed series of her own life under this message.
- **Our edge is proof, not the slogan.**

## Episode structure (every day, 30–60s)
1. **Hook (0–3s):** "Day 7 of Zero Kobo From Him. Today's rent money came from ___." The verdict comes first.
2. **Proof (3–30s):** one slice of the real work: a client call, an ad dashboard with figures blurred, a course sale notification with the amount blurred, a grocery run with the price on screen.
3. **One lesson (30–50s):** the skill behind it, in one sentence.
4. **Closing question:** "Would you date a man who never gave you money? Yes or no."

## Safety rules (Port Harcourt)
- Never show:
  - cash or bank balances
  - account numbers
  - your street, estate, car plate or house exterior
- Post after you've left a location, not while you're there.
- No real-time check-ins.

## First 7 episode themes
1. Day 1: the claim, with no receipts yet. Let them demand receipts.
2. Day 2: what a digital marketer actually does all day.
3. Day 3: rent day. "Who paid it?" (show the process, not the amount)
4. Day 4: a date where you paid for yourself. Their reaction is the content.
5. Day 5: the delayed receipt. Reply to the angriest "show receipt" comment.
6. Day 6: "The skill I'd teach my younger sister so she never has to collect."
7. Day 7: "A week of Zero Kobo From Him. Here's what it cost me." (honest numbers, blurred)
