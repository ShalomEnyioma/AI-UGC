## Changes from Peace's raw opinion (and why)
1. **Cut "I don't understand women."**
   - Saying it makes women the enemy, and they're your main audience. It also reads as "pick-me".
   - The rule is to aim at the *logic*, not at women. The line already does that: "For wetin exactly?"
   - If you really want it, use "I don't understand this logic" instead.
2. **Reworded "once he sees you're bearing his children".**
   - The original wording could be clipped and shared as "she's telling girls to get pregnant to get paid".
   - The new version says responsibility starts with marriage and children. Same meaning, no hostage line.
3. **Kept "contact sport".**
   - This is the line that will start the war in the comments: if both of you enjoy it, money turns it into a transaction.
   - It's an opinion, not a lie, so it passes the integrity test.
   - Keep the euphemism. The explicit word gets the video restricted.
4. **Added the bride-price line and the series plug.**
   - Bride price is the money lens, and it's very Nigerian.
   - The plug sends viewers to the series, and it starts the delayed-receipt setup.
